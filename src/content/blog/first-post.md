Welcome to my very first blog post! I am officially building out my personal portfolio site to showcase my growth as a developer.

## Why Astro?
I chose Astro because it is incredibly lightweight. It compiles everything down to clean, static HTML, which means the performance scores will be fast enough to meet the project's strict criteria.
